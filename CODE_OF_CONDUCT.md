# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a good experience for everyone,
whatever their background or level of experience.

## What we expect

Be kind and assume good faith. Accept that people disagree, and argue about the
work rather than the person. When someone points out a mistake, take it as help.

Harassment of any kind is not acceptable. Neither is personal abuse, publishing
someone's private information, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
somebody is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory or by contacting the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may
block anyone who keeps breaking them.
